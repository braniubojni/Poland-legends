# Content rules

This app teaches a language. It does not teach how to live in a country.

## Allowed

- Polish phrases and accepted answers
- EN and RU glosses
- One short grammar or usage rule
- One optional culture note that helps you *speak* (formality, greeting time of day)
- Everyday scenes: cafe, street, home, travel, small talk

## Forbidden

- Urząd, ZUS, NFZ, MOS, visas, residence cards, taxes, lawyers
- Document checklists, fees, deadlines presented as current procedure
- Party politics
- Copy written for Polish citizens daily civic needs
- Mixing this course with a future citizen app

## Voice

International learner. Adult. Clear. AirLearn-like explanations, not slang dumps and not only textbook I-am-an-apple lines.
