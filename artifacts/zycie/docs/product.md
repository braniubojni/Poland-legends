# Product

Working name: **Życie** (folder only). Public name later. Do not bikeshed in scaffold prompts.

## What this is

A free, international language trainer. Closest references: **AirLearn** (teach first, then practice, light culture) and **Duolingo** (short sessions, streak, drills). Vocab pacing like **Drops**.

## Audience (v1)

Anyone who wants to learn Polish as a foreign language. Tourists, hobby learners, partners, future residents — treated as *language learners*, not as people navigating Polish offices.

## Audience (explicitly not v1)

- Expats who need MOS / Blue Card / ZUS walkthroughs
- Polish citizens looking for a local super-app

Those ideas stay off this roadmap.

## Loop (v1)

1. Open app (English UI, optional Russian).
2. Pick a unit: Greetings, Café, Directions, Family, Food, …
3. 4–6 minute session: vocab cards → one grammar/use rule → drills.
4. Streak + SRS in IndexedDB.
5. Done. No account required.

## First course

Polish A0–A1. Everyday language. Culture as seasoning, not the meal.

## Success

A stranger finishes three units on a phone with no server and no Polish-administration knowledge.

## Non-goals (v1)

- 12 languages like AirLearn’s catalog
- Legal or “how to live in Poland” content
- Voice calls, livestream translation
- Community / civic features for Poles
